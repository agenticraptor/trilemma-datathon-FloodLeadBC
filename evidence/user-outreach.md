## Message

> Hi — I'm a Vancouver developer building a free, open tool for the Trilemma Foundation × Northeastern Datathon. It watches the federal river gauges near Sumas Prairie and Chilliwack and estimates, in hours, when a river will reach the level at which a farm needs to start moving animals, then calls you and texts your helpers once you approve. Could I have 15 minutes of your time this week to hear how the 2021 and 2025 floods actually unfolded on your farm? I'm trying to learn what warning would have helped, not to sell anything.

## Interview questions (15 minutes)

1. Walk me through the night the water came in 2021 or 2025. When did you first decide to act?
2. What told you to act — a siren, an alert, a neighbour, the river itself?
3. How many hours did moving your animals (or equipment) take? Who helped?
4. What information did you wish you had, and how many hours earlier?
5. Would you trust a probability ("70% chance your field floods by 4 a.m.")? What would make you trust it?
6. If a tool called you and then texted your helpers after you said yes, would you use it? What would make you turn it off?
